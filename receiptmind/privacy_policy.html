1. Introduction

Welcome to ReceiptMind: Expense Tracker ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures and commitments regarding data collection, usage, and security for the ReceiptMind mobile application.

ReceiptMind is engineered on an Offline-First, Privacy-by-Design architecture. Your financial data belongs strictly to you and is processed and stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Camera & Photos: We request camera access exclusively when you choose to scan a physical receipt or invoice. Images are processed locally on your device using on-device optical character recognition (OCR) and are never uploaded to any external server without your explicit command.
  • Financial Records: Expense entries, store names, line items, and transaction amounts are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device (using AES-256 encryption).
  • Location & Device Identifiers: We do NOT collect, track, or monitor your GPS location, advertising ID (GAID), or device serial numbers.
  • Analytics & Tracking: ReceiptMind contains zero third-party advertising trackers and zero third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

3. Data Storage & Security

We implement bank-grade encryption to protect your sensitive data:

  • All database records are protected with local AES-256 encryption.
  • Your data remains on your physical device unless you explicitly choose to export it (e.g. to CSV, PDF, or encrypted backup).
  • You have the ability to permanently delete any individual receipt or reset your entire database at any time from the app's Settings menu.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your financial data, receipts, or personal details with any third parties or data brokers.

5. Children's Privacy

ReceiptMind is designed for general audiences and individuals aged 18 and older managing personal finances. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
